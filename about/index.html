About The Corporate Closet

What this is

Office outfit ideas and workwear essentials for the corporate girlie. Affordable business casual looks, capsule wardrobe staples and style inspiration, with every outfit broken down into the pieces you can shop.

How the pieces are chosen

Affordable where possible, versatile, and office-appropriate. The palette is neutral, so pieces mix with each other and one purchase works in many outfits.
